What I work on

My work sits where estimation, simulation and control meet: algorithms that run on real robots, and the tools to test them safely first.

State estimation and sensor fusion

Relative localization for drone and ground-robot teams, fusing UWB, radar, IMU and wheel odometry with factor graphs.

  • Radio-based multi-robot localization ROS 2, C++. Fuses UWB ranging and radar ego-motion with odometry in a pose graph to find where the drone is relative to the ground robot. On real data, the drone's trajectory error is about 1.2 % of the distance flown. First-author paper published at ICRA 2026.

    Real-world dataset from the ICRA 2026 paper: drone and ground robot moving together, with UWB, radar and odometry on board (1.5× speed).

    Code · Paper · Video
  • Ceres and GTSAM pose-graph backends Added a GTSAM 4.3 backend next to Ceres behind a solver-independent interface. In early benchmarks it gives identical results 1.5–2× faster, at about 2 ms per optimization step. Code · Write-up
  • Open UAV-UGV radio dataset The real-world ROS 2 bag behind the paper: a ground robot with four UWB anchors and a 4D radar, and a drone with two UWB tags and its own radar, with LiDAR-based ground truth. Open under CC BY-SA 4.0.

    LiDAR map of the test hall, used for the ground-truth trajectories.

    Dataset · How to use it

Robotics simulation

Simulators and Software-in-the-Loop pipelines that make field work faster and safer.

  • UWBPX4Sim UWB ranging (TWR and TDoA) for PX4 drone and rover fleets in Gazebo, calibrated on three real-world datasets, obstacles and materials included. Presented at ICRA 2026 and ROSCon Spain 2026.

    Replaying a real UTIL dataset flight, with cabinets blocking four anchors.

    Building scenario in UWBPX4Sim, with UWB crossing walls: a ground robot explores the rooms while a drone flies the hallway and outside. With UWB, the ground robot's error stays near 0.8 m, against 3.2 m with odometry alone.

    Repository
  • HuNavSim for Webots Plugin that brings simulated pedestrians into Webots for human-aware navigation. Repository
